About

I am a research scientist at Reality Labs Research, Meta. My current work focuses on egocentric contextual AI for always-on wearable devices such as smart glasses — long-horizon video understanding, personal memory, and multimodal models grounded in a user's everyday visual stream. Earlier, my research centered on visual localization, learned local feature descriptors, scene understanding, and the privacy and security of visual data in AR / VR systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, advised by Jan-Michael Frahm.
